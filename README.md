# Repeated domestication shaped the evolution of grain amaranth in Mesoamerica.
The domestication of grain amaranths is a complicated and not yet fully understood process. Amaranthus cruentus was domesticated in Mesoamerica; however, its diversity and history remain vastly unknown. In this study, we conducted population genomic analyses using whole genome sequencing from A. cruentus as well as the relatives A. hybridus and A. hypochondriacus. Our results reveal patterns of genomic structure, diversity, and introgression consistent with multiple A. cruentus populations that evolved partially independent despite the geographic proximity.
